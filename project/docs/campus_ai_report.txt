Campus AI Adoption Report 2026 (sample document for the Research Assistant Agent)

Summary
A survey of 5 departments at a engineering college studied how students use AI tools for studying.
In total 1,250 students were surveyed. 68% reported using an LLM chatbot at least weekly.
The most common use was explaining concepts (41%), followed by debugging code (33%) and
summarising notes (18%). Only 8% used AI to write full assignments.

Findings
1. Students who used AI for concept explanation improved their average test score by 6.5 points.
2. Departments with a written AI-use policy reported 40% fewer academic integrity cases.
3. The Electronics & Computer Engineering department had the highest adoption rate at 81%.

Recommendations
- Introduce a clear AI-use policy in every department.
- Run a workshop on prompt writing and verifying AI answers.
- Allocate a budget of Rs. 2,50,000 for campus-wide AI tool licences; the expected number of
  users is 1,250 so the target cost per student must stay below Rs. 250.
